Northstar's support-drafting assistant calls a hosted model provider; nobody at Northstar trained the model. When drafts slow down and the bill spikes, why is this LLMOps rather than MLOps, and why does the SRE stack not catch it?

gateway-diff.sh compares routing.yaml against routing-baseline.yaml and returns 'review-required' with support_cost_delta_per_day_usd of 8946 for a support route that moved fast → deep. What is the design point of pricing a one-line route change this way?

You split a support request trace and see provider_ms: 8390, application_ms: 340, dominant: provider. A teammate says 'let's optimize the application code to fix the latency.' Why is that the wrong move?

The support route's rate_limit.tpm is 300000 and was never edited during the incident, yet llm-dials.sh now reports tpm_headroom_pct: -150. How did an unchanged rate-limit line become a violation?
