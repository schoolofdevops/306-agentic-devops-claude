The structural analyzer flags a one-line plan that shows `['delete','create']` on an `aws_db_instance` as a block, while passing a fifty-resource plan of pure creates. What principle is this?

Blast radius, as the reviewer measures it, is best described as which of the following?

Drift analysis diffs a candidate plan against a known-good baseline. What specific risk is this designed to catch?

The FinOps rule flags any single resource that grows past a 2x threshold. Why is a per-resource multiple more useful than only reporting the total monthly delta?

Why is the IaC Engineer the only role in the chain that carries a write tool, with Security, FinOps, and Change reviewers having none?

The agent-run cost receipt compares the mixed-model chain against running every role on Opus. What is the receipt meant to demonstrate?
